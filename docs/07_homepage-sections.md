# Vivabox --- Homepage Progress

> **Status:** Advanced / validated architecture **Scope:** First three
> homepage sections **Last updated:** July 2026

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. Current Homepage Progress

The first three sections of the homepage are now strategically defined:

1.  ✅ **Hero**
2.  ✅ **¿Qué incluye una Vivabox? + ¿Y después?**
3.  ✅ **Ejemplos de experiencias**

Together, these sections move the buyer through three mental states:

> **Entiendo la idea.**
>
> ↓
>
> **Entiendo exactamente qué estoy regalando y qué pasa después.**
>
> ↓
>
> **Veo que hay suficiente variedad para que encuentre algo que le
> guste.**

Each section has one primary psychological job. The homepage must not
repeat information unnecessarily.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. Hero

## Status

✅ **Validated**

## Psychological Mission

Make the Vivabox idea understandable almost immediately.

The Hero introduces the fundamental benefit: the buyer does not have to
correctly guess which experience another person will prefer. The
recipient gets to choose.

## Headline

> **Tú regalas, ellos eligen.**

## Supporting Copy

> **Regalas una Vivabox.**
>
> **Quien la recibe descubre y elige la experiencia que más le
> emociona.**

## CTA

Primary:

> **Comprar Vivabox**

Secondary:

> **Cómo funciona**

The secondary CTA can scroll toward the explanatory content further down
the homepage. A separate standalone "Cómo funciona" section is no longer
required.

## Desired Mental Outcome

After the Hero, the buyer should understand:

-   Vivabox is a gift.
-   The buyer does not choose one specific experience.
-   The recipient chooses later.
-   Choice is part of the value proposition.

Desired reaction:

> **"Qué buena idea de regalo."**

## Do Not

-   Explain activation in detail.
-   Show a large activity catalogue.
-   Make Vivabox look like a marketplace.
-   Introduce unnecessary technical information.
-   Overload the first screen with reassurance arguments.

The Hero creates **understanding and interest**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 3. ¿Qué incluye una Vivabox? + ¿Y después?

## Status

✅ **Validated as one combined section**

## Strategic Decision

There will **not** be another standalone "¿Cómo funciona?" section
immediately afterward.

The lower part of this section --- **¿Y después?** --- already performs
that function.

The combined section answers two consecutive questions:

### ¿Qué incluye una Vivabox?

> **¿Qué estoy regalando realmente?**

### ¿Y después?

> **¿Qué pasa cuando la persona la recibe?**

Together, they provide the complete product explanation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 4. ¿Qué incluye una Vivabox?

## Psychological Mission

Make the invisible visible.

The buyer should feel that they are being shown what is inside the gift
before giving it.

This creates:

-   transparency;
-   reassurance;
-   perceived value;
-   understanding;
-   curiosity.

Desired conclusion:

> **"Sé exactamente qué estoy regalando."**

## Visual Principle

The central visual is an **open Vivabox**.

Its contents are partially visible and identified using the Vivabox
graphic language:

-   hand-drawn arrows;
-   handwritten annotations;
-   small illustrated icons;
-   warm cream / paper background;
-   navy typography;
-   restrained Vivabox accent colors;
-   realistic product representation;
-   generous breathing room.

The result should feel editorial, warm and playful --- not like a
technical diagram.

# 6. What Does Not Belong as a Fourth Box Content

Do not position:

> **Vivabox se encarga**

as another physical item surrounding the box.

Vivabox coordination belongs to the service after activation, not to the
physical contents.

It therefore belongs naturally in **¿Y después?**

This also avoids repetition.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 7. ¿Y después?

## Psychological Mission

Answer the natural question:

> **"Ok, entiendo lo que recibe. ¿Y después qué pasa?"**

This block replaces the need for a separate "Cómo funciona" section.

The process must feel extremely simple.

## Intro

> **¿Y después?**
>
> Activa su código y descubre el catálogo completo en línea.

A smartphone visual can show the Vivabox online experience, ideally with
the map and category markers visible.

This communicates the transition from physical gift to digital
discovery.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 11. Ejemplos de experiencias

## Status

✅ **Validated direction and existing carousel structure**

## Psychological Mission

At this point the homepage stops explaining mechanics.

The buyer already understands Vivabox.

Now the objective is **projection and reassurance through variety**.

Desired reaction:

> **"Hay suficiente variedad. Seguro encuentra algo que le guste."**

The experiences are proof of variety, not products the buyer is expected
to compare and purchase individually.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 12. Section Title

The current title:

> **Algunas experiencias disponibles**

should evolve because "disponibles" can make the section feel like a
live marketplace catalogue.

Preferred direction:

> **Muchas formas de disfrutar una Vivabox**

Supporting copy:

> **Ejemplos de experiencias en Bogotá y Cundinamarca.**

This keeps the section inspirational while clearly communicating that
the displayed experiences are examples.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 13. Carousel Structure

The existing horizontal carousel is retained.

It works particularly well on mobile because:

-   it is naturally swipeable;
-   the next card can remain partially visible;
-   it creates curiosity;
-   it communicates variety progressively;
-   it avoids displaying an overwhelming catalogue.

The homepage should show a limited curated sample rather than a large
grid.

Recommended category coverage:

-   Gastronomía
-   Bienestar
-   Aventura
-   Cultura
-   Estancias

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 14. Experience Card Information

Recommended hierarchy:

1.  Strong experience photograph
2.  Category pill
3.  Experience name
4.  Location

Example:

> **Aventura**
>
> Sobrevuelo panorámico de la ciudad en avión
>
> Bogotá

Avoid unnecessary booking-style metadata.

Duration should only appear if it provides meaningful differentiation.
Repeated information such as "60 min" makes the interface feel more like
a booking marketplace.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 15. Provider Visibility

The homepage should **not prominently identify the provider** behind
each experience.

This is intentional.

The buyer purchases Vivabox and the freedom of choice it creates --- not
a specific provider.

Keeping provider visibility secondary:

-   keeps Vivabox as the central product;
-   prevents marketplace comparison behavior;
-   reduces dependency on individual partner names;
-   protects flexibility as the catalogue evolves;
-   avoids turning changing partnerships into perceived broken promises.

The experience is presented first.

The provider remains part of the operational layer behind it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 16. Experience Modal

The modal can provide additional context, but it must not become a
marketplace product page.

Its role is:

> **Help the buyer understand what this type of experience could feel
> like.**

Not:

> **Help the buyer decide whether to book this exact experience.**

Recommended content:

-   experience image;
-   category;
-   experience name;
-   location;
-   short evocative description;
-   concise clarification that it is an example within the evolving
    Vivabox selection.

Suggested clarification:

> **Ejemplo de experiencia Vivabox. La selección puede evolucionar.**

Avoid excessive booking-style metadata unless genuinely useful.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 17. Final Carousel Card

The final card communicates that the displayed experiences are only a
sample.

Preferred direction:

> **Y muchas más por descubrir**
>
> La selección Vivabox evoluciona constantemente.

This creates continuity with the discovery idea introduced in the
previous section and prevents the visible carousel from being
interpreted as the complete catalogue.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 18. No Experience Catalogue CTA

Do not add a CTA such as:

> **Ver todas las experiencias**

or:

> **Explorar experiencias**

to this homepage section.

That would encourage the buyer to analyse individual experiences and
recreate the decision anxiety Vivabox is designed to remove.

The buyer should not need to inspect the entire catalogue before
trusting the gift.

The section succeeds when they conclude:

> **"Hay suficiente variedad para que encuentre algo que le guste."**
