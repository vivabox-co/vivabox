# Purpose

This document defines the role, structure and UX principles of the Vivabox product page (`/regalar`).

It serves as the reference for designers, developers and AI assistants.

This page exists between the Homepage and the Checkout.

Its purpose is **not** to repeat the Homepage.

Its purpose is **not** to explain the entire Vivabox concept.

Its purpose is to transform purchase intent into purchase decision.

---

↓

**/regalar**

↓

↓

Activation

The customer already understands what Vivabox is.

The product page now answers one single question:

> **"Is this the product I want to buy?"**

---

# Role of the page

The Homepage sells the idea.

The Product Page sells the product.

The Checkout completes the purchase.

These three pages must never compete with each other.

---

# Main UX Goal

Increase purchase confidence immediately before checkout.

The page should answer every remaining objection while keeping the user emotionally engaged.

It should feel closer to an Apple product page than to a traditional marketing landing page.

---

# Design Principles

## Product first

The physical Vivabox is now the hero.

Not the concept.

Not the experiences.

Not the booking system.

The visitor must clearly understand:

"This is the gift I am buying."

---

## Minimal repetition

The Homepage already explains:

- what Vivabox is;
- how it works;
- examples of experiences;
- social proof.

The Product Page should avoid repeating these sections unless repetition adds reassurance.

Reassurance is acceptable.

Redundancy is not.

---

## Calm and premium

Large white spaces.

Few sections.

Large photography.

Little text.

One idea per section.

Never overload the page.

---

## Buyer-oriented

The page speaks to the buyer.

Not to the recipient.

Every section should answer:

"Why is this a great gift to give?"

## ¿Por qué funciona tan bien como regalo?

Purpose

Explain why Vivabox solves the biggest gifting problem.

Not by explaining the product.

By explaining the buying benefit.

Three cards.

---

Card 1

Siempre aciertas.

No tienes que adivinar.

La persona descubre todas las posibilidades y elige la que más le emociona.

---

Card 2

El regalo se vive dos veces.

Primero al abrir la caja.

Después al disfrutar la experiencia.

---

Card 3

Ella elige.

No importa si le gusta la gastronomía, el bienestar o la aventura.

Ella decide.

---

Purpose

These cards sell confidence.

Not features.

# Section 3

## Así de simple.

Purpose

Show how natural the experience feels.

Only three steps.

Large photography.

Very little text.

---

Step 1

Regalas

Beautiful image of someone offering the Vivabox.

Short copy.

---

Step 2

Descubre

Opening the Vivabox.

Showing the printed catalog.

---

Step 3

Vive

Beautiful real experience.

Minimal copy.

# Section 4

## Algunas experiencias que podría elegir

Purpose

Reconnect the visitor with the real value of Vivabox.

This is not a catalog.

Only examples.

---

Display

Maximum four experience cards.

One per category.

Example:

Gastronomía

Bienestar

Aventura

Estancias

(or Cultura)

---

No filters.

No search.

No large carousel.

No marketplace feeling.

Only enough to help visitors imagine the possibilities.

# Section 5

This is not a help center.

Only high-impact questions.

---

¿Tiene vencimiento?

Sí.

---

¿Hacen envíos a toda Colombia?

Sí.

# Relationship with the Homepage

Homepage answers:

Why Vivabox?

Product Page answers:

Why this Vivabox?

The Homepage creates desire.

The Product Page creates certainty.

---

# Relationship with Checkout

The Product Page sells.

The Checkout should never sell again.

After clicking:

REGALAR UNA VIVABOX

the visitor enters transaction mode.

The Checkout should therefore:

- ask only necessary information;
- avoid repeating marketing;
- avoid explaining Vivabox again.

✓ The product.

✓ The price.

✓ What is included.

✓ How gifting works.
